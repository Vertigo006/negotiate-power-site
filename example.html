How should increased business-aircraft demand be met?

Acquire · Fractional · Charter · Wait

THE CALL

WAIT

Demand has increased, but the available evidence does not yet establish that the increase will persist long enough to justify the additional commitment of ownership.

WHAT IF WE'RE WRONG?

Commitment changes the downside

If utilization normalizes, acquisition creates greater lock-in and a longer recovery path than more flexible alternatives.

WHAT CHANGES THE CALL?

Persistence plus economics

Sustained utilization and verified ownership economics sufficient to overcome the value of remaining flexible.

Why this matters

The cheapest path can become the most expensive decision.

Expected cost matters. So does the cost of being wrong. BRENO tests whether the consequences of an assumption failing are large enough to change the preferred path.

The recommendation is only useful if the buyer can inspect the evidence basis, uncertainty, alternatives, and conditions that should reopen the call.
