Should we put our aircraft on charter?

Charter revenue can offset ownership cost, but the right question is not how much the aircraft can gross. It is what reaches the owner after variable cost, maintenance, availability conflicts, wear, management economics, and operating constraints.

Direct answer

Charter is supportable when incremental net benefit is meaningful and the added utilization does not compromise the owner's primary mission or create disproportionate maintenance and availability exposure.

NET ECONOMICS

Follow the money to the owner

Model gross charter revenue less operator share, fuel, maintenance accruals, engine and APU programs, crew, repositioning, cleaning, handling, commissions, and incremental wear.

AVAILABILITY

Price the conflict with owner use

Test peak periods, short-notice missions, return positioning, recovery, maintenance downtime, and whether charter commitments reduce the access that justified ownership.

OPERATING MODEL

Understand who controls the charter operation

Separate the owner's Part 91 use from the certificate holder's Part 135 operational control, responsibilities, commercial terms, and eligibility requirements.

Beyond the Brief

“Offset the fixed costs” can hide a weak ownership thesis.

If the ownership model only works when charter utilization is aggressive, the deeper question may be whether the aircraft should be owned in the first place. Stress the economics at lower charter volume and higher maintenance burden before treating revenue as dependable.

Decision discipline

Use specialists for specialist conclusions. Use decision intelligence to reconcile what their conclusions mean together.

Technical, legal, tax, financing, maintenance, operational, and regulatory questions should be addressed by qualified professionals. BRENO does not replace those experts. It structures the decision around their evidence, competing alternatives, assumptions, uncertainty, and the consequences of being wrong.

Beyond the Brief

Resolve the decision you brought. Then test whether it is the right decision to be making.

BRENO creates an inspectable basis for the requested decision, then separately challenges framing, alternatives, dependencies, and assumptions that may materially change the direction.
