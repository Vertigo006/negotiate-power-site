What happens after you contact us

A defined path from inquiry to decision support.

The process is designed to establish fit, scope, timing, and cost before custom production begins.

01

Send the decision

Email the decision, when it must be made, the alternatives under consideration, and what is most at stake. Do not send sensitive records in the first message.

02

Confidential qualification

A focused discussion determines whether Negotiate Power can materially improve the decision and which engagement depth fits the problem.

03

Fixed scope and price

The exact decision, alternatives, evidence boundary, deliverables, delivery target, and fixed engagement price are confirmed before custom production begins.

04

Controlled production

Atlas structures the relevant evidence, preserves material contradictions and unknowns, and supports comparison within the agreed scope.

05

Human-reviewed release

The decision asset is reviewed before release and delivered as a controlled, versioned output designed to support accountable judgment.

06

Decision debrief

The engagement closes with a confidential discussion of the recommendation, evidence strength, remaining uncertainty, and the triggers that could change the call.
