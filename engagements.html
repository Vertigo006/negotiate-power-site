How the challenge works

Atlas does not need to disagree with you to produce a useful result.

The commercial unit is the integrity of the challenge, not the number of problems found.

Baseline

Preserve the pre-Atlas position

Record the decision, current conclusion, material assumptions, alternatives, timing, and what the normal professional process already considered.

Challenge

Test for decision-material change

Examine evidence, contradictions, constraints, uncertainty, and the conditions that could change diligence, confidence, timing, or the decision.

Result

Report change or confirmation

Atlas may identify a new diligence requirement, condition, confidence change, timing change, or decision change. If nothing material changes within the defined evidence boundary, that is recorded as a valid result.

Operating boundary

Context can carry forward. Conclusions cannot.

A prior recommendation, evidence state, confidence level, or release authorization is never assumed to remain valid for a later decision. New material evidence, changed constraints, stale information, deadlines, and commitment points trigger reassessment.

No subscription price is currently published. Ongoing support is qualified and scoped based on the expected decision stream and current authorized case pricing.

What happens next

From existing professional judgment to a bounded independent challenge.

Qualification establishes fit, preserves the pre-Atlas position, identifies repeat-decision demand, and fixes scope before any authorized custom work begins.

01

Bring the decision and current position

Provide the question, decision owner, deadline, alternatives, what is most at stake, and—where practicable—what your normal professional process currently concludes. Do not send sensitive records in the first email.

02

Qualify fit and Decision Inventory

We confirm the decision class, evidence and rights boundary, timing, analytical depth, current professional process, and whether additional consequential decisions are expected in the next 30–90 days. Repeat-demand classification is used for commercial prioritization only.

03

Confirm scope, timing, price, and release requirements

The exact question, evidence cutoff, permitted use, exclusions, delivery target, applicable data handling, human-review responsibilities, fixed price, and required release conditions are confirmed before authorized custom work begins.

04

Close the decision and identify the next trigger

When a case is authorized for customer-facing release, record what changed—or did not change—plus the reassessment trigger and next likely Decision Object when one exists. A second engagement begins only when another decision is independently qualified.
