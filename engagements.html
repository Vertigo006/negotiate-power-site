What the buyer receives

A decision asset, not access to AI.

Decision frame

The question, alternatives, evidence, and material assumptions

The output defines what is being decided, what alternatives remain viable, what evidence is material, and what assumptions or unknowns constrain the analysis.

Decision analysis

Contradictions, uncertainty, exposure, and recommendation discipline

Material conflicts and unresolved gaps remain visible. Where supported, the analysis can include risk and economic exposure, a recommendation or disposition, conditions, and the evidence that would change the call.

Accountability

Evidence references, explainability, and human-review boundaries

Consequential conclusions are presented so the decision owner can inspect why the evidence supports the direction, what remains unresolved, and what requires further verification or qualified human judgment.

Scope and price

Final scope is confirmed before production begins.

Starting prices provide an immediate budget reference. Final scope and fixed engagement price are confirmed after confidential qualification.

01

Scope factors

Analytical complexity, evidence requirements, number of alternatives, urgency, and any separately authorized third-party data needs can affect final pricing.

02

Rescoping trigger

If the decision question, alternatives, evidence boundary, deadline, permitted use, or required deliverables materially change, production pauses and the engagement is rescoped rather than silently expanded.

03

Controlled boundary

No sensitive records are required for the initial qualification discussion. Records are requested only after fit, authority, permitted use, and scope are established.

What happens after you contact us

A defined path from real decision to governed decision support.

The process establishes competence, evidence sufficiency, authority, scope, timing, and cost before custom production begins.
