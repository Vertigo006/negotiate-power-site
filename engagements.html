Qualification boundaries

Some inquiries should be held, rescoped, or declined.

Qualified

One real bounded decision

An accountable owner, identifiable alternatives, decision date, meaningful consequences, lawful evidence access, and a scope Atlas can support without crossing professional boundaries.

Hold / rescope

The decision or evidence boundary is not ready

The question is too broad, alternatives or owner are unclear, required evidence is expected later, or the timing and effort require a narrower scope.

Decline

The requested determination belongs elsewhere

Atlas does not provide principal legal, tax, appraisal, underwriting, airworthiness, maintenance, safety, regulatory-certification, or other excluded professional determinations, nor guaranteed outcomes.

What happens next

A defined path from active decision to governed decision support.

Initial inquiries target a response within one business day. A qualifying case then moves through confidential qualification and fixed-scope confirmation before production.

02

03

Fixed scope and price
