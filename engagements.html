Pricing

Three levels of analytical depth.

Public starting prices are qualification anchors. Final scope and fixed price are confirmed before production begins.

Decision Brief

Starting at $2,500

Best for: one consequential aviation decision requiring focused independent analysis.

Typical planning range: 3–5 business days after scope and required inputs are complete.

Focused recommendation, viable alternatives, decisive evidence, uncertainty, contradictions, exposure where supportable, reversal conditions, and next actions.

Decision Intelligence Assessment

Starting at $5,000

Best for: a consequential decision involving multiple meaningful alternatives, dependencies, or competing evidence.

Typical planning range: 5–7 business days after scope and required inputs are complete.

Adds deeper comparison, dependencies, protections, evidence treatment, and decision-relevant operator or market analysis where needed.

Decision Intelligence + Forward Cost

Starting at $7,500

Best for: decisions where downstream exposure, lock-in, persistence, recovery timing, or exit consequences can change the preferred path.

Typical planning range: 7–10 business days after scope and required inputs are complete.

Adds forward-cost and recovery analysis where the evidence supports quantification or bounded estimation.

Planning ranges are not guarantees. Actual delivery timing is confirmed during qualification and fixed-scope approval and may vary with evidence availability, complexity, urgency, third-party dependencies, customer-record handling, or required specialist review.

What happens next

From active decision to fixed scope.

The initial inquiry establishes fit, scope, timing, data requirements, and price before custom analysis begins.

01

Bring the decision

Provide the question, decision owner, deadline, alternatives, and what is most at stake. Do not send sensitive records in the first email.

02

Qualify the fit

We confirm the decision class, alternatives, evidence and rights boundary, timing, analytical depth, and preliminary scope. Atlas declines work that requires unsupported capability or excluded professional determinations.

03

Confirm scope, timing, and price

The exact question, evidence cutoff, permitted use, customer-facing output, exclusions, delivery target, applicable data handling, human-review responsibilities, and fixed price are confirmed before production.
