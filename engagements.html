What the buyer receives

A decision asset, not access to AI.

The exact deliverable depends on scope, but every engagement is organized around the question the customer must decide.

Decision frame

The question, alternatives, evidence, and assumptions

Defines what is being decided, what alternatives remain viable, and what evidence or assumptions constrain the analysis.

Decision analysis

Contradictions, uncertainty, exposure, and disposition

Surfaces material conflicts and unknowns and, where justified, provides a recommendation or disposition with conditions that could change the call.

Accountability

Evidence references and human-review boundaries

Shows why the evidence supports the direction, what remains unresolved, and what requires further verification or qualified human judgment.

Engagement comparison

Choose the depth the decision requires.

Starting prices and delivery windows are shown below. Final scope and fixed price are confirmed before production begins.

Decision Brief

Starting at $2,500

Best for: one bounded consequential question.

Typical delivery: 3 to 5 business days.

Primary deliverable: concise decision framing, evidence strength, material contradictions and unknowns, and a recommendation, abstention, or second opinion where justified.

Core engagement

Decision Intelligence Assessment — starting at $5,000

Best for: multiple viable paths across broader evidence domains.

Typical delivery: 5 to 7 business days.

Primary deliverable: broader comparison package, including a Decision Brief, Decision Matrix, relevant intelligence profile where applicable, and explicit uncertainty treatment.

Forward Cost

Decision Intelligence + Forward Cost — starting at $7,500

Best for: decisions where downstream exposure, lock-in, or recovery timing can change the preferred path.

Typical delivery: 7 to 10 business days.

Primary deliverable: full assessment plus Forward Cost & Recovery Analysis.

Controlled commercial release. A limited number of qualifying first-time engagements may be eligible for founding-client terms. Qualification is determined during scope review.
