# Release 3B — Independent Aviation Second-Opinion Funnel

Date: 2026-09-25
Status: STAGED ONLY — DO NOT MERGE before Release 3A clears its production gate and is stable

## Hypothesis

Some high-intent aviation decision owners already have a proposed recommendation, aircraft, provider, transaction, ownership structure, fleet plan, or operating path. They may not identify with a generic new-decision intake, but may value an independent governed challenge before commitment.

## Existing asset decision

`challenge.html` is currently a noindex legacy bridge from the Atlas-era challenge concept to BRENO. It is therefore the lowest-risk place to stage the second-opinion funnel without creating a new URL, changing navigation, or exposing it to search before the sequential gate is satisfied.

The staged page remains `noindex,follow`. No sitemap or navigation change is included. Publication/indexation is a later evidence-gated decision.

## Commercial boundary

This pathway does not create a new product or price. A tightly bounded second opinion may fit the existing Decision Brief; more complex cases may require the existing Decision Intelligence Assessment. Suitability remains scoped case by case.

## Claim control

Supported: independent structured challenge of evidence, assumptions, contradictions, alternatives, uncertainty, exposure, and change conditions; preservation of an inspectable Decision Asset; accountable human authority.

Explicitly not claimed: that BRENO will find an error, prove an adviser wrong, outperform advisers, guarantee a different answer, replace qualified professional diligence, or autonomously make the decision.

## Measurement

The staged page uses `aviation_second_opinion` as its coarse decision category and a distinct `Aviation Second Opinion` email subject. Stage 0 records only CTA type, coarse category, and originating path.

## Gate

Do not open a PR, trigger Vercel, merge, index, add to navigation, or otherwise publish this staged branch until Release 3A has cleared its required preview and production verification and the sequential commercial directive permits Release 3B.
