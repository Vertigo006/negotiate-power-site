Privacy

Privacy notice

Updated September 1, 2026

Negotiate Power operates this informational website to describe Atlas aviation decision intelligence and provide a direct channel for inquiries. This notice describes the limited information processed through the current public website and inquiry workflow.

Current inquiry path

The website does not currently use an on-site inquiry form. Qualification links open your own email application and address a message to contact@negotiatepower.com. The message is transmitted by your email provider and received in Negotiate Power's Microsoft-hosted business mailbox. Do not send sensitive records, credentials, medical information, government identifiers, or other restricted information in an initial inquiry unless Negotiate Power has separately established an appropriate channel and requested it.

Information you provide

If you email us, we may receive your name, email address, organization, decision question, decision owner, timing, alternatives, primary concern, and any other information you voluntarily include. Initial qualification is intended to begin with high-level decision information rather than sensitive supporting records.

Information collected automatically

Negotiate Power does not currently use advertising cookies, paid analytics, behavioral tracking, an on-site submission form, or a customer account system on this public site. The website host and internet service providers may process routine technical information such as IP address, browser/device information, request time, and requested page for security, reliability, and service delivery.

How inquiry information is used

Inquiry information may be used to respond to you, determine whether the decision is an appropriate fit for Atlas, schedule qualification, define scope and evidence boundaries, maintain business records, protect systems, and comply with applicable obligations. An initial inquiry does not create an engagement; any engagement requires separate scope, terms, and authorization.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by providers that support website hosting, email delivery and mailbox storage, domain/DNS operations, security, or professional advice, and may be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or system integrity. The current public site is hosted through GitHub Pages, and the current inquiry mailbox is Microsoft-hosted.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Website inquiry messages are stored in the business email system and may remain in mailbox folders or related business records until they are deleted or retained for a legitimate business, security, dispute, or legal purpose. Negotiate Power has not published a fixed retention period for initial inquiries. You may request correction or deletion by emailing contact@negotiatepower.com; some information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal, security, or recordkeeping purposes.

Cookies and analytics

No Negotiate Power analytics or advertising cookie is currently set by the site's own implementation. If analytics, forms, payment processing, customer accounts, or other material data collection are added later, this notice should be updated before or when those features are activated.

Security

The current inquiry flow uses ordinary internet email and should not be treated as a secure evidence-transfer channel. No claim is made here that email is end-to-end encrypted, that a dedicated secure portal exists, or that the public website provides regulated-data handling. Reasonable administrative and provider controls may be used, but no email or internet transmission can be guaranteed secure.

Children

This website is intended for business and professional audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be revised as the website, services, and data practices develop. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

Privacy questions, correction requests, and deletion requests may be sent to contact@negotiatepower.com.
