How scope changes

Move up a tier when the decision becomes harder to bound, not because you want more pages.

The engagement level is driven by the decision itself: number and quality of credible alternatives, evidence depth, contradictions, material dependencies, exposure, and whether downstream cost or recovery changes the call.

Negotiate Power recommends the lowest level that can resolve the decision without stripping out material complexity.

What is outside scope

BRENO supports the decision. It does not replace professional authority.

Where a decision requires legal, tax, appraisal, maintenance, engineering, regulatory, underwriting, investment, or other qualified professional judgment, BRENO may incorporate the relevant professional evidence and analysis into the decision record, but does not replace the qualified professional or authority responsible for that judgment.
