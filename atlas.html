The commercial architecture

One decision object. One governed evidence boundary. One inspectable decision record.

Negotiate Power is the company. Atlas is the product. Atlas Engine is the governed analytical capability used to produce the work.

The process begins by defining the Decision Object: who is deciding, what must be decided, the alternatives, deadline, constraints, evidence boundary, and permitted use.

Decision Object→Evidence→Contradictions + unknowns→Recommendation→Decision Asset

How Atlas behaves

Controls buyers can see in the output.

The customer does not need to read the proprietary methodology to inspect the consequential reasoning.

Evidence provenance

Claims remain connected to evidence

Relevant evidence is bounded to the Decision Object. Material source lineage remains available for review.

Contradiction handling

Conflict stays visible

Conflicting evidence is not silently averaged away. It is resolved, bounded, or carried forward as uncertainty.

Unknowns

Missing evidence remains missing

Absent verification, conflicting evidence, and affirmative adverse evidence are treated as different states.

Confidence

Confidence is bounded to evidence

Atlas avoids manufactured numeric precision and can withhold a recommendation when the evidence cannot responsibly support one.

Falsification

What could change the call is explicit

Material assumptions, counterarguments, reversal triggers, and required verification remain part of the Decision Asset.

Human authority

Support does not become authority

Atlas does not autonomously approve, release, execute, or substitute for consequential authority reserved to accountable people or qualified professionals.

AIOQ v1.0

Institutionally Qualified — Bounded.

Atlas completed AIOQ v1.0 and was designated INSTITUTIONALLY QUALIFIED — BOUNDED for the tested methodology and decision classes.

AIOQ tested whether Atlas could preserve its institutional decision process under controlled independent execution, including unseen generalization, regression, competence-boundary, adverse-authority, and release-governance challenges.

What AIOQ does not prove: that every recommendation is correct; future outcomes; universal model accuracy or portability; regulatory approval; autonomous authority; customer ROI; product-market fit; or predictive superiority.

Data & methodological governance

Progressive disclosure, not proprietary-methodology exposure.

Customer-facing work shows the decision boundary, evidence references, contradictions, unknowns, confidence, falsification conditions, and human-review state. Proprietary schemas, scoring logic, source architecture, and internal control implementation remain private.

Engagement scope also defines customer-record boundaries, rights and confidentiality state, evidence cutoff, permitted use, and release responsibilities before production.

For current public data-handling terms, review the Privacy notice. Specific nonpublic data requirements are addressed during confidential qualification and scope.

Decision Delta

Qualification measures process. Decision Delta measures change.

AIOQ and Decision Delta answer different questions. AIOQ tests whether Atlas preserves its institutional controls. Decision Delta is the governed method for recording whether and how a real decision changed after Atlas.

Where available, Atlas can compare the locked pre-Atlas baseline with the post-Atlas decision across recommendation, evidence, risk, confidence, time, economic exposure, governance, and outcome. Attribution remains bounded; null, negative, and ambiguous results are preserved.

No aggregate Decision Delta performance statistic is currently claimed.

What Atlas is not

Boundaries are part of the product.

Atlas is not a generic AI assistant, chatbot, aviation news service, dashboard, aircraft marketplace, appraisal service, public safety-rating system, regulator, accredited certification body, or autonomous decision-maker.

It does not replace qualified legal, tax, financial, insurance, maintenance, engineering, regulatory, operational, investment, or other professional authority.
