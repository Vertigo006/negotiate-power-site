Why this page remains public

Product names can change. Evidence should not.

Qualification artifacts, case records, governance decisions, and historical claims must remain attributable to the identity and controls under which they were created. Negotiate Power therefore does not relabel Atlas-era evidence as though it originated under BRENO.

BRENO is the current customer-facing identity. Atlas remains a provenance marker wherever the historical record requires it.

Atlas-era method

The core discipline that preceded the BRENO identity.

Decision definition

Bound the choice before analyzing it

Identify the decision owner, alternatives, deadline, constraints, evidence boundary, and conditions under which the answer must remain valid.

Evidence discipline

Keep provenance, gaps, and contradictions visible

Separate fact from inference, preserve source lineage, and do not convert missing or conflicting evidence into false certainty.

Challenge & release

Test the answer, then retain human authority

Examine alternatives, uncertainty, exposure, and reversal conditions while keeping consequential release and customer authority with accountable humans.

AIOQ v1.0

Institutionally qualified, bounded.

Under the Atlas identity, Negotiate Power completed a controlled institutional qualification program designed to test evidence handling, reasoning discipline, uncertainty, competence boundaries, and human-release controls across bounded aviation decision cases.

The recorded result was INSTITUTIONALLY QUALIFIED — BOUNDED for the tested methodology and decision classes.

That result did not establish universal recommendation accuracy, future outcomes, regulatory approval, autonomous authority, customer ROI, product-market fit, production eligibility by itself, or predictive superiority.

Decision Delta

Outcome evidence remains bounded too.

The Atlas-era Decision Delta framework was designed to compare a customer's pre-analysis position, the recommendation, the actual customer decision, and observed outcome when sufficient prospective evidence exists.

It can preserve changes in decision position, uncertainty, evidence, risk treatment, timing, exposure, optionality, and outcome without converting ambiguous or unsupported observations into performance claims.

No aggregate Decision Delta performance statistic or ROI claim is made here.

Current continuity

What carried forward is the discipline, not a retroactive rename.

BRENO's current public methodology continues to emphasize bounded decisions, inspectable evidence, visible uncertainty, explicit change conditions, competence boundaries, and accountable human authority.

For current product information, use the BRENO pages. For historical Atlas qualification context, this page remains the provenance record.
