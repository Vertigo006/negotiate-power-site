BRENO

The decision layer

Resolve the decision before commitment makes the answer expensive.

A consequential decision may already have a financial model, market data, internal analysis and expert advice behind it.

Having the inputs and resolving the decision are different problems.

BRENO is Negotiate Power's proprietary decision-intelligence system. It is applied through scoped engagements to determine which path is supportable now, what happens if a critical assumption fails, and what should cause the call to change.

Existing expertise stays

You don't need to replace the experts.

Your broker may know the market. Your operator may know the mission. Your attorney may know the contract. Your technical adviser may know the asset. Your finance team may know the economics.

BRENO asks a different question: What does all of that mean for this decision?

Negotiate Power brings decision-relevant inputs together without silently assuming authority that belongs to the customer or a qualified professional.

Systemized decision work

The output is more than a recommendation.

BRENO bounds the decision, compares credible alternatives, keeps source lineage and contradictions visible, tracks critical assumptions and unresolved unknowns, examines commitment and recovery exposure, and identifies the conditions that should reopen the call when the evidence supports them.

The result is an inspectable decision record, not a black-box answer.

The cost of being wrong

Expected economics are only part of the decision.

An attractive forecast can depend on an assumption that has not yet been established. BRENO tests whether commitment, lock-in, recovery time and downstream exposure change the preferred path when that assumption is stressed.

The question is not only which option looks best. It is what happens if the reason it looks best turns out to be wrong.

The cheapest-looking path can become impressively expensive after you sign it.

How an engagement works

Negotiate Power operates BRENO against your live decision.

You bring the decision, decision owner, commitment point, credible alternatives and what is most at stake. Negotiate Power qualifies the fit, defines the evidence boundary and scope, and applies BRENO to the decision.

You receive an inspectable analytical result and decision record. The accountable decision remains with you.

Scope

Aviation is BRENO's proving ground.

Negotiate Power's deepest demonstrated work remains aviation. The underlying decision problem also appears elsewhere: organizations must reconcile alternatives, evidence, constraints and consequences before committing.

BRENO can be applied to selected non-aviation decisions when the necessary domain evidence, expertise and controls can be established for the specific decision.
