The commitment moment

The expensive part is not having another opinion. It is committing before the decision has been challenged.

Atlas is designed for a narrow moment: your advisers and internal process have produced a position, the commitment is consequential, and you want an independent test before the point becomes difficult or expensive to reverse.

CONFIRM

The decision survives

No additional decision-material issue changes the current direction within the defined evidence boundary. Assumptions, uncertainty and reassessment triggers remain explicit.

CONDITION

The decision survives with conditions

The underlying direction remains supportable, but added verification, diligence, protection or a defined condition becomes necessary before commitment.

CHANGE

The decision no longer survives as formed

Material evidence or a constraint changes the preferred path, confidence, timing or decision.
