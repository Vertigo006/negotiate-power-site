Decision Delta

Measure what changed after the analysis.

Where the engagement permits, Atlas captures the pre-Atlas decision baseline and later records whether the recommendation, evidence, risk treatment, confidence, timing, economics, governance, or outcome changed. This governed measurement method is called Decision Delta.

No aggregate performance or ROI claim is implied. Decision Delta is the measurement architecture used to learn what changed and why as prospective outcomes accumulate.
