Who is behind Negotiate Power

Built from aviation operations, commercial judgment, and accountable decision-making.

Negotiate Power was founded by Michael Ward, a commercial pilot, flight instructor, aviation operations leader, and former business founder. Atlas grew from a practical problem: consequential aviation choices often require evidence from different sources, competing commercial interests, and judgment under uncertainty.

Aviation is Atlas's proving ground. The objective is not to replace experienced advisers or decision owners; it is to give them a more inspectable decision record before commitment.
