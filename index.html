The decision problem

High-stakes aviation decisions rarely fail for lack of data.

They fail because the relevant evidence is fragmented across records, entities, time periods, and source types—leaving decision-makers to reconcile material facts under pressure.

Negotiate Power is being built to reduce that burden: separating signal from noise, exposing uncertainty, and presenting the findings that materially change the decision.

The approach

Finished intelligence, not another dashboard.

Our work is organized around the decision to be improved—not the volume of information that can be displayed.

01

Evidence discipline

Material claims are tied to identifiable sources, temporal boundaries, and confidence conditions.

02

Decision clarity

Outputs emphasize the recommendation, differentiated finding, supporting evidence, and unresolved risk.

03

Controlled confidence

Contradictions, missing evidence, and analytical limits remain visible rather than being compressed into false certainty.

What Atlas produces

Decision products built around the question that matters.

Evidence to decision

The lowest immediate cost is not always the lowest-cost decision.

A conventional comparison can identify the cheapest option today. Atlas is designed to examine what follows: contractual lock-in, mitigation dependencies, normalization timing, residual expense, and the conditions that could shorten or extend the recovery tail.

The objective is not false precision. It is to make the economic consequences of each decision path explicit before the decision is made.
