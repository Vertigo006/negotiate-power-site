High-intent decision guides

Start with the question you actually need to answer.

AIRCRAFT ACQUISITION

Should we buy an aircraft?

Test ownership against charter, fractional access, blended access, and optimization before committing capital.

CAPACITY

Do we need another aircraft?

Separate a real capacity constraint from utilization, scheduling, mission-mix, or access-model problems.

ACCESS MODEL

Charter, fractional, or ownership?

Compare the access models on mission coverage, economics, flexibility, control, and lock-in.

MANAGEMENT

Should we change management companies?

Evaluate whether dissatisfaction is a provider problem, contract problem, operating-model problem, or expectation gap.

TIMING

Should we delay an aircraft purchase?

Test whether waiting preserves option value or simply postpones a decision the evidence already supports.

How to use these

These are decision frameworks, not substitute professional opinions.

Each guide identifies the evidence that matters, the assumptions most likely to distort the answer, and the conditions that could reverse the direction. They are designed to help a decision owner frame the problem before bringing in deeper diligence.
