Purpose

Clarity before commitment.

This library is written for people making economically meaningful aviation decisions. It does not manufacture a universal winner. It identifies the conditions that change the answer, distinguishes evidence from inference, and makes the consequences of being wrong easier to see.

Decision discipline

A number is an input. The consequence is the decision.

Quoted rates, annual hours, fleet counts, and management fees can make two alternatives appear comparable. Their behavior under peak demand, downtime, operating restrictions, organizational change, contractual lock-in, or early exit is what separates them.
