Purpose

Useful guidance before custom analysis.

This library is written for aircraft owners, advisers, flight departments, operators, and other decision owners. It does not manufacture a universal winner. It identifies conditions that change the answer, separates evidence from inference, and makes the consequences of being wrong easier to see.

When a live decision depends on your specific mission, alternatives, counterparties, contracts, timing, or evidence, BRENO provides the decision-specific layer.

When public guidance stops being enough

Your decision has facts the article cannot know.

A public framework can identify the right questions. It cannot know your mission, evidence, alternatives, counterparties, contract terms, timing, constraints, or tolerance for being wrong.

BRENO is the decision-specific layer. It reconciles those inputs into an inspectable call, exposes the material downside, and identifies what should change the call when the evidence supports it.
