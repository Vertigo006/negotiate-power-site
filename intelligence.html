Purpose

Clarity before commitment.

This library is written for aircraft owners, advisers, flight departments, operators, and other decision owners evaluating economically meaningful aviation choices. It does not manufacture a universal winner. It identifies the conditions that change the answer, distinguishes evidence from inference, and makes the consequences of being wrong easier to see.
