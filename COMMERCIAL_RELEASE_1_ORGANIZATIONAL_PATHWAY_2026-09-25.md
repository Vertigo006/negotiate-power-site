# Release 1 — Organizational / Repeat-Decision Pathway

Date: 2026-09-25
Status: implementation candidate

## Hypothesis

Qualified aviation organizations with recurring consequential decisions may interpret the current BRENO site as supporting only isolated one-off engagements. A restrained organizational pathway may surface broader fit without commercializing subscriptions, memberships, Workbench seats, unlimited use, or unsupported recurring-service promises.

## Claim classification

- Organizations can face recurring consequential aviation decisions: directly supportable market/customer framing.
- BRENO engagements preserve an inspectable basis for the decision being evaluated, including material evidence, assumptions, contradictions, alternatives, and change conditions: directly supported by current public positioning and current governed Decision Workbench snapshot/decision architecture.
- Negotiate Power can evaluate whether a broader organizational engagement is appropriate when needs recur: supported with qualification. This is an invitation to scope an engagement, not a claim that a recurring product or monitoring service exists.
- Subscription, membership, enterprise license, Workbench seat, unlimited decisions, SLA, continuous monitoring: DO NOT CLAIM and explicitly not offered by this release.

## Smallest viable change

One homepage section immediately before existing engagement/pricing cards. One CTA: `Discuss an Organizational Use Case`. It uses the existing email intake architecture with a distinct subject so Stage 0 analytics can classify it as `organizational_use`.

No pricing, product catalog, navigation architecture, Workbench behavior, or methodology disclosure changes.

## Experiment

Primary signal: organizational-use CTA clicks distinguished from ordinary commercial CTA clicks.

A click is interest, not qualification or commercial validation. Qualified conversations, engagements, and revenue must be reconciled from authoritative operational records rather than inferred from analytics.

## Next action after production verification

OBSERVE this pathway. Do not rewrite or expand it merely because deployment succeeds. Release 2 is a separate lifecycle hypothesis and requires its own claim-control inspection.
